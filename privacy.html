Loadout — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

In short

Loadout has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. Everything you enter stays on your own device. We operate no server and receive no data from you whatsoever — not your meals, not your body stats, not your photos.

What Loadout stores, and where

All of your information is kept in your browser's local storage, on the device you are using. It is never uploaded anywhere. This includes:

  • Character details you enter: age, sex, height, bodyweight, activity level and goal
  • Your calorie and macronutrient targets
  • Meal plans, prep schedules, pantry contents and shopping lists
  • Journal entries recording what you ate
  • App preferences such as your chosen theme

Because this data lives only on your device, we cannot see it, retrieve it or restore it. If you clear your browser data or use the app's Start over command, it is permanently gone.

Barcode photographs

When you photograph a barcode, the image is decoded entirely on your device. It is drawn to an in-memory canvas, read for a barcode number, and discarded. The photograph is never uploaded, never transmitted and never stored. Only the resulting barcode number — a short string of digits — is used, and only to look up that product.

Loadout requests no camera permission and captures no live video. It only reads an image file that you explicitly choose.

Third-party services

Loadout does not send your personal data to anyone. However, like any web page, loading it makes network requests, and the operators of those services will see your IP address. These are the only external connections the app makes:

ServiceWhenWhat it receives
Open Food Facts Only when you scan a barcode or search for a food The barcode number or search term, plus your IP address
jsDelivr Only when a barcode decoder is needed (always on iPhone) Your IP address, to deliver the decoder file

These services are operated independently of Loadout and handle your IP address under their own privacy policies. No identifying information about you is attached to any of these requests by Loadout.

Typefaces are served by Loadout itself. Many web apps load their fonts from Google, which discloses every visitor's IP address to Google on every page load. Loadout does not: the fonts are included with the app, so no request is made to Google and nothing about you reaches them. If you never scan a barcode, the app makes no external network requests at all.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, receive, store or process any personal data
  • We do not use cookies or any similar tracking technology
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services
  • We do not show advertising
  • We do not sell or share personal information — we have none to sell
  • We do not create accounts or profiles
  • We do not request your location

Your rights

Privacy laws including the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data that a company holds about you. Loadout holds none, so there is nothing for us to disclose or erase. You retain complete and direct control of your own copy: it is on your device, and Start over in the System menu erases all of it immediately.

Children

Loadout is a general-audience fitness tool and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

Health disclaimer

Loadout provides general fitness estimates for informational purposes only. It is not medical, clinical, or professional dietary advice, and it is not a medical device. Calorie and macronutrient figures are approximations produced by standard formulas, and nutrition data supplied by Open Food Facts is contributed by volunteers and is frequently incomplete or inaccurate — always check the packaging. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before changing your diet, particularly if you are pregnant, under 18, or have any medical condition or eating disorder.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Because the app collects no data, any change will concern how the app itself behaves rather than how information about you is handled.
