Loadout — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

In short

Loadout has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. Everything you enter stays on your own device. We operate no server and receive no data from you whatsoever — not your meals, not your body stats, not your photos.

What Loadout stores, and where

All of your information is kept in your browser's local storage, on the device you are using. It is never uploaded anywhere. This includes:

  • Character details you enter: age, sex, height, bodyweight, activity level and goal
  • Your calorie and macronutrient targets
  • Meal plans, prep schedules, pantry contents and shopping lists
  • Journal entries recording what you ate
  • App preferences such as your chosen theme

Because this data lives only on your device, we cannot see it, retrieve it or restore it. If you clear your browser data or use the app's Start over command, it is permanently gone.

The camera and barcode scanning

Loadout can read a barcode two ways: with your camera, or from a photo you choose. Both are decoded entirely on your device — each frame is drawn to an in-memory canvas, read for a barcode number, and discarded. No image, frame or video is ever uploaded, transmitted or stored. Only the resulting barcode number — a short string of digits — leaves the device, and only to look up that product.

The live scanner asks for camera permission when you open it, and the camera runs only while the scanner is on screen. Nothing is recorded, and no video ever reaches us or anyone else. If you decline the permission, have no camera, or your browser cannot support live capture, Loadout falls back to reading an image file that you explicitly choose. You can withdraw camera access at any time in your browser or system settings.

The Health app

In the iPhone app only, Loadout can fill in your character sheet from Apple Health so you do not have to type it, and show your step count on your character sheet. It reads five things and nothing else: biological sex, date of birth, height, the most recent bodyweight, and your step count. Loadout never writes anything to Health, and never reads workouts, heart rate, medications, cycle tracking, or any other category.

This happens only when you tap Import from Health, never automatically and never in the background. iOS asks your permission first, per value, and you can refuse any or all of them — anything you decline is simply left for you to type in. The imported numbers are then treated exactly like numbers you typed: they stay on your device, and are covered by everything said above. You can withdraw access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health. The website version cannot read Health at all.

Third-party services

Loadout does not send your personal data to anyone. However, looking a product up means making a network request, and the operator of that service will see your IP address. This is the only external connection the app makes:

ServiceWhenWhat it receives
Open Food Facts Only when you scan a barcode or search for a food The barcode number or search term, plus your IP address

These services are operated independently of Loadout and handle your IP address under their own privacy policies. No identifying information about you is attached to any of these requests by Loadout.

Open Food Facts is now the only external connection the app makes. The barcode decoder used to be fetched from the jsDelivr CDN when it was needed; it is now included in the app itself, so nothing is requested from jsDelivr and it never sees your IP address.

Products you have already scanned are remembered on your device, so scanning the same item again is answered from your own phone without asking Open Food Facts a second time. That store holds product information only — a name and its nutrition against a barcode — never anything about you, and like everything else it stays on the device. It is capped at the 300 most recent products, and clearing the app's data removes it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, receive, store or process any personal data
  • We do not use cookies or any similar tracking technology
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services
  • We do not show advertising
  • We do not sell or share personal information — we have none to sell
  • We do not create accounts or profiles
  • We do not request your location

Your rights

Privacy laws including the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data that a company holds about you. Loadout holds none, so there is nothing for us to disclose or erase. You retain complete and direct control of your own copy: it is on your device, and Start over in the System menu erases all of it immediately.

Children

Loadout is a general-audience fitness tool and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

Health disclaimer

Loadout provides general fitness estimates for informational purposes only. It is not medical, clinical, or professional dietary advice, and it is not a medical device. Calorie and macronutrient figures are approximations produced by standard formulas, and nutrition data supplied by Open Food Facts is contributed by volunteers and is frequently incomplete or inaccurate — always check the packaging. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before changing your diet, particularly if you are pregnant, under 18, or have any medical condition or eating disorder.
