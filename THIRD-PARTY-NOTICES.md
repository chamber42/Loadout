## Data

### Open Food Facts

Product and nutrition information for scanned barcodes and food searches comes
from Open Food Facts (https://world.openfoodfacts.org/), used under the
**Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0**. https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/

Loadout queries the Open Food Facts API at runtime and displays the results. It
does not redistribute the database or any adapted copy of it, so the app is a
"Produced Work" under the ODbL rather than a Derivative Database. Product
photographs, where shown, are licensed CC-BY-SA by their contributors.

**If a snapshot of Open Food Facts data is ever bundled into the app, that
bundled database falls under the ODbL's share-alike terms and this notice must
be revised accordingly.** The bundled table in `js/42-usda-data.js` is drawn
from USDA and contains no Open Food Facts data, precisely so that this stays
true.

### U.S. Government nutrition data

Nutrient reference values, food composition data, and the bundled food table
searched alongside Open Food Facts are drawn from works of the United States
federal government, which are not subject to copyright in the United States
(17 U.S.C. § 105) and are in the public domain:

- USDA FoodData Central — https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/
  - Foundation Foods (2026-04-30)
  - SR Legacy (2018-04)

`js/42-usda-data.js` bundles 7,832 foods from those two datasets — name,
calories and five nutrients per 100 g. It is generated by `tools/build-usda.js`
from the published bulk downloads and can be regenerated at any time. Because
these works are public domain, the table carries no licence, requires no
attribution and imposes no share-alike obligation, which is what makes it
safe to bundle where the Open Food Facts database is not.

FDC's **Branded Foods** dataset is deliberately excluded: Open Food Facts
covers packaged products better and more currently, and the bundled table
exists to fill the gap Open Food Facts leaves at raw and whole foods.
- U.S. Food and Drug Administration Daily Values (21 CFR 101.9)
- National Institutes of Health, Office of Dietary Supplements

No attribution is required for these sources, and their use here does not imply
endorsement by the USDA, the FDA, the NIH or any other agency.
